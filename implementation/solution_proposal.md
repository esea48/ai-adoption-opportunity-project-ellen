## Executive Summary

Cleo's company operates in a sector facing a structural staffing crisis, rising administrative burden, and accelerating regulatory digitalisation requirements. The fastest, most evidence-backed lever available — requiring no clinical risk, no regulatory overhaul, and no disruption to resident care — is deploying AI to automate shift documentation.

**Recommended Solution:** Deploy **Voize** (Berlin-based AI documentation tool) as the first AI investment.

**Why:** Voize is already deployed in 1,100+ German and Austrian care facilities, saves caregivers ~30 minutes per shift, costs €12–€15 per user per month, runs on existing smartphones, and was founded in Berlin by a team that built it specifically for the German care context.

**Expected Year 1 ROI (Berlin-specific):**
- Annual value of recovered staff time: **~€105,800**
- Total Year 1 cost: **€10,700–€24,800**
- Payback period: **4–8 weeks**

## 1. Problem Statement

Based on sector benchmarks for comparable German care companies, Cleo's organisation is almost certainly experiencing all three of the following simultaneously:

### 1.1 Documentation Consumes Caregiver Time
Nurses in German care homes spend up to 30% of every shift on documentation — vital sign logs, medication records, incident reports, care plan updates. That is roughly 2–2.5 hours of a standard shift spent at a screen rather than with a resident.

### 1.2 Burnout Is Driving Staff Turnover
62% of healthcare workers cite administrative overload as the single greatest driver of burnout. In Berlin, replacing one care worker costs an estimated 50–100% of their annual salary in recruitment, onboarding, and training — a direct operational cost.

### 1.3 Germany's Legal Environment Is Accelerating Pressure
Since July 2025, all care facilities in Germany are legally required to connect to the Telematikinfrastruktur (TI). The DVPMG framework means digital tools are now actively funded by the Pflegekassen. Facilities that build digital capability now gain access to subsidy programmes; those that delay will face this transition under greater pressure.

## 2. Proposed Solution

### 2.1 Tool: Voize — AI Documentation Companion for Nurses

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Vendor** | Voize GmbH |
| **Headquarters** | Berlin, Germany (founded 2020) |
| **Technology** | Ambient AI speech recognition — caregiver speaks naturally; Voize structures and files the documentation |
| **Language** | German-native; handles dialects, non-native speakers, care-specific terminology |
| **Integration** | Bi-directional EHR integration; ePA/TI compatible |
| **Infrastructure** | Runs offline on existing smartphones — no Wi-Fi dependency, no hardware purchase |
| **GDPR** | Fully compliant; EU-hosted; local data processing |
| **Deployment scale** | 1,100+ care facilities; 75,000 nurses in Germany and Austria |
| **User retention** | 96% continue using daily after onboarding |
| **Staff adoption** | Most staff prefer it within days of first use |

### 2.2 How It Works (For Non-Technical Stakeholders)

A caregiver finishes washing a resident and says aloud: *"Frau Schmidt — Morgenpflege abgeschlossen, Blutdruck 130/85, keine Auffälligkeiten."* Voize hears this, maps it to the correct documentation fields in the ePA, and files it — in seconds, without the caregiver touching a computer.

It is the difference between a caregiver spending 30 minutes at a terminal at the end of a shift versus speaking three sentences during the shift itself.

### 2.3 What It Does Not Do

Voize does not:
- Diagnose residents or make clinical decisions
- Add or assume information not spoken by the caregiver
- Replace the caregiver's judgment — every entry requires caregiver review/approval before filing

This is critical for staff trust and regulatory compliance. There is always a human in the loop.

## 6. Assumptions

This proposal is based on sector benchmarks in the absence of internal company data. The following assumptions apply:

1. The company has basic smartphone/tablet access for frontline caregivers
2. The company is connected to, or in the process of connecting to, the TI (legally required since July 2025)
3. Staff turnover is at or near the German care sector average (~30%/year), making retention ROI a valid secondary metric
4. No internal AI or data science team exists — solution must be vendor-led and low-maintenance
5. A Betriebsrat (works council) may exist given the 150–200 headcount; early engagement is recommended
6. Cleo's priority is demonstrable value within 12 months, not a multi-year transformation roadmap

> **Note:** These assumptions should be validated at the first formal meeting with Cleo. Any significant deviation (e.g., existing digital infrastructure, active Betriebsrat, pre-existing vendor relationships) may affect the recommendation.
