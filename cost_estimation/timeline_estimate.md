Total duration from kick-off to full deployment: **18–22 weeks (~5 months).**

| Milestone | Target |
|---|---|
| Kick-off & stakeholder alignment | Week 1 |
| Betriebsrat notification sent | Week 2–3 |
| 2-week time-tracking exercise complete | Week 3 |
| Device policy confirmed | Week 3 |
| AVV / DSGVO legal review complete | Week 4 |
| voize contract signed | Week 4–5 |
| MEDIFOX DAN API integration complete | Week 6–7 |
| Pilot cohort onboarded and live | Week 7–8 |
| Mid-pilot structured review | Week 10 |
| Pilot sign-off | Week 12 |
| Cohort 1 rollout complete (~35 caregivers) | Week 14–15 |
| Cohort 2 rollout complete (~35 caregivers) | Week 17–18 |

| 3-month post-rollout review | Month 8 |

## Schedule

| Weeks | Activity |

| 10 | Mid-pilot structured feedback collected, adjustments made |

**Months 4–5 — Full Rollout**

| Weeks | Activity |
|---|---|
| 11–14 | Cohort 1 onboarding and go-live (~35 caregivers) |
| 15–18 | Cohort 2 onboarding and go-live (~35 caregivers) |

**Month 6+ — Embed & Phase 2 Preparation**

| Month | Activity |
|---|---|
| 6–7 | Monthly team lead reviews, documentation quality monitoring |
| 6–7 | Begin Phase 2 scoping: confirm waitlist / client demand for Tour Planning ROI |
| 8 | 3-month post-rollout review — re-measure documentation time vs. baseline |

---

**Schedule risks to monitor:**

- **Device procurement**: if company smartphones are required and not yet available, hardware lead times of 2–4 weeks can delay the pilot start. Begin procurement in Week 1.
- **AVV legal review**: external counsel availability may extend the Week 4 deadline. Engage counsel in Week 1 in parallel with other discovery activities.
- **API integration complexity**: MEDIFOX DAN integrations have varied in complexity across Pflegedienste deployments. Allow a 1-week contingency buffer.
- **Caregiver onboarding scheduling**: coordinating 35-person cohort sessions around shift patterns requires 2–3 weeks of advance planning.

> **Phase 2 trigger:** Phase 2 (Tour Planning AI) scoping should begin no later than Month 6 of Phase 1, conditional on confirming that CareNest has unmet client demand to fill freed scheduling capacity. Without a confirmed waitlist, Phase 2 ROI does not materialise.
